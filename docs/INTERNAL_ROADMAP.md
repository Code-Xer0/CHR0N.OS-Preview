# Internal Roadmap Boundary

CHRON.OS is part of the Hyperion local-first intelligence stack. The public build focuses on archival intelligence. The deeper stack is intentionally staged so the public beta can stay honest, safe, and understandable.

## Public Build

The public lane is the Windows archival intelligence beta:

- local archive intake
- provenance-preserving copy workflows
- file and context inspection
- chronological sorting
- search-oriented organization
- archive telemetry and aging signals
- non-mutating source behavior
- durable photo, video, and audio intake
- approved watched sources and native Quick Intake
- bounded OCR, scene analysis, and optional local transcription
- installer, MSI, portable ZIP, and checksum distribution

## Staged Direction

The following lanes describe direction, not public-shipped guarantees:

- MNEM.OS continuity bridge
- Nest automation dispatch
- Cerberus telemetry proposals
- policy automation
- retention workflows
- environment memory
- cloud/NAS hardening
- production OAuth
- speaker diarization
- production multilingual OCR packs
- deeper media models and unbounded processing profiles

These areas may appear as read-only surfaces, prototypes, proposal language, internal tests, or staged integrations before they become public product features.

## Product Posture

The clean public label is:

**Public Beta / Local-First Preview Lane**

The ecosystem label is:

**Part of the Hyperion local-first intelligence stack. Public build focuses on archival intelligence. Interop lanes remain staged.**

## What Not To Claim

Do not claim that CHRON.OS is finished, enterprise production-ready, autonomous records governance, or a replacement for legal, medical, financial, or safety-critical review.

Do not imply cloud sync, destructive retention, or third-party automation is active by default.

Do not expose private archive screenshots, internal doctrine, live customer data, credentials, or unreleased source paths in public docs or marketing.
